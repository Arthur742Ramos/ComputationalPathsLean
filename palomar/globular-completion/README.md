# Relative globular completion of computational paths

This development isolates a precise construction from the computational-path
programme: freely add higher comparison syntax to a fixed path/rewrite
2-skeleton, without identifying the lower-dimensional data.

The result is a **relative globular completion**, not a formalization of the
full weak omega-groupoid theorem. The identity-type comparison in the accepted
manuscript's Theorem 10.2 is explicitly excluded.

## The mathematical statement

Start with a layer consisting of objects, arrows, and source and target maps.
Two arrows are parallel when both endpoints agree. Form a new layer whose
objects are the old arrows and whose arrows are expressions generated by:

1. an identity expression at each arrow;
2. a chosen generator between each parallel pair;
3. inversion of an expression;
4. composition of expressions with matching intermediate endpoints.

The generator takes a parallel-boundary proof and equality of the two witnesses
of `Nonempty L.Arr`. This adapts the paper's truncation-based premise to a
generic layer. The equality premise is automatic in Lean. Its role is a **declared choice of
completion**, not a proof that the original rewriting theory was coherent.

The main results are:

- Every generated expression has a parallel boundary, and every parallel
  boundary admits one. Iterating the construction gives a genuinely recursive
  globular tower.
- Each extension has a unique interpretation in any indexed target with the
  four displayed operations. The target need not obey associativity or inverse
  cancellation. This is freeness of **syntax**, not freeness as a groupoid.
- Boundary-preserving maps extend functorially: identity and composition are
  respected, as are syntax-node counts.
- The computational-path application fixes dimensions zero through two
  definitionally. Its two pentagon routes are unequal 2-cells, with step counts
  two and three, but a 3-cell joins them. Adding comparison data does not
  silently quotient away the original derivations.

The selected declarations and exact axiom footprints are in
`formalization.yaml`. `SOURCE_MAP.md` explains the relationship to the manuscript
and earlier Lean files.

## Why this scope matters

Researchers studying formal higher rewrite systems need to distinguish three
different claims: the existence of a chosen higher filler; coherence derived
from rewrite rules; and equality of the underlying derivation records.
This development separates them in a reusable, typed construction, characterizes
the construction by a layerwise interpretation property, and checks that
lower-dimensional syntax remains distinguishable.

This is a modest foundational and corrective contribution. The universal
property uses structural induction; no novelty claim is made for the general
free-syntax technique. The research-interest case is the precise construction,
its reuse on arbitrary layers, and the clarified computational-path application,
not the size of the proof files or the acceptance of the source paper.
Palomar's editorial assessment remains separate.

## Scope limits

- Path is a trace-decorated ambient equality. Its list entries are not
  certified rewrite steps or an inductive encoding of a type theory.
- Step is an explicit seven-constructor fragment, not the complete native
  LND_EQ-TRS.
- We do not construct an action of a contractible globular operad, prove a
  Batanin-Leinster weak omega-groupoid theorem, or identify this tower with
  Martin-Lof identity types.
- Higher vertical operations and comparisons are provided. No complete
  all-dimensional horizontal-composition or whiskering structure is claimed.
- The interpretation property is for each extension layer. A global initiality
  theorem in a category of infinite towers is not claimed.
- No confluence, normalization completeness, circle/HIT application or
  fundamental-group theorem is selected.

These limits are part of the statement of record, not deferred proof obligations
for a stronger advertised theorem.

## Reproduce

With Lean 4.33.0 available through elan:

```bash
bash scripts/check.sh
bash scripts/verify-comparator.sh   # Linux; includes independent NanoDa replay
```

The standalone Challenge and Solution import only Mathlib.Data.List.Basic.
Both contain the same complete development, deliberately duplicated so that
the statement has no project-local import and neither module needs proof
holes. This is transparent duplication, not an independent second proof.
The roughly 500-line statement is below Palomar's hard limit but exceeds its preferred
300-line audit envelope. Short statements and proofs are kept together for
inspection rather than hiding the completion semantics behind opaque values.

The original failed boundary remains in the sibling weak-omega-groupoid
directory for provenance; it is not this submission's Comparator surface.
Verification of this package does not approve that earlier boundary.
